# Airtel SYNC demo deployment

All applicant records, registry entries, IDs, businesses and document images in `data/` are fictional competition specimens. They are publicly served as static demo data. The prototype performs OCR in the browser and does not authenticate a real person or place an order.

Tesseract.js and its language/core assets are bundled locally under `vendor/tesseract/`. See the included license files for attribution and license terms.
